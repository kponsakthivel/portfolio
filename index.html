About me

The person behind the systems: how I think, how I work and what I care about.

I'm K.ponsakthivel, a Solution Engineer at Vintrosys Business Solutions in Tiruppur. I design and deliver ERP business solutions using Frappe and its products, across Buying, Selling, CRM, Helpdesk, Manufacturing, Accounting, HRMS and Education, for businesses in industries like textile manufacturing and retail.

I joined Vintrosys as a techno-functional intern, grew into a Junior Techno Functional Specialist working on development, implementation, customization, testing, support and end-user training, and now work as a Solution Engineer.

My work sits between people and systems. With business teams, I sit down to understand how their work actually happens and what they need from it. Then I shape the solution in Frappe and ERPNext so it fits the way they work.

I can handle a project single-handedly, from the first requirement conversation to delivery. I have also led a team and managed projects, coordinating the people, the plan and the client along the way.
